# Floor QR checkpoints (superseded)

Floor-only scanning has been replaced by [room QR checkpoints](room-qr-checkpoints.md). Use **Scan QR** for Computer Labs 1-5, EMC Laboratory, and Rooms 501-505. The five manual floor buttons and ordinary floor deep links remain available.

Old floor codes are no longer accepted by the scanner. New printable codes are at `/room-qr/print.html`. See the room checkpoint guide for current assets, behavior, and verification commands.
