# Floor QR Checkpoints Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement task-by-task. Execution is already authorized in chat; work in the existing clean QR-CODE feature branch and leave a reviewable diff.

**Goal:** Scan one of five floor QR codes and display that floor on the existing indoor map.

**Architecture:** A shared floor-link validator feeds the map and session-scoped auth handoff. A lazy-loaded camera scanner decodes locally and releases its camera on every exit. Existing map state resets when a validated checkpoint is applied.

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, React Router, qr-scanner, node-qrcode, Capacitor Android, node:test, Playwright.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-floor-qr-design.md

## Global Constraints

- Exactly five floor destinations: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th.
- Floor only: no positioning, new route, database changes, or external app links.
- Preserve manual floor selection, authentication/approval guards, and current offline behavior.
- Camera is user initiated, decodes locally, and stops on close/background/navigation/success.
- No deployment, account changes, or unrelated UI redesign.

## Review Focus

- Reject foreign URLs, malformed/duplicate floors, and unrelated QR content without navigating.
- Same-floor repeated scans reset the map once per scanner session; invalid scans do not lock it.
- Close/background during permission or decoder initialization never leaves a camera stream running.
- Login and pending approval retain the floor without bypassing access checks.
- Manual selection still works after scanning and after reloading a floor link.
